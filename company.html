Circle Five Studios is a technology company building apps that bring people together. We focus on social products designed around real relationships, not follower counts.

Our name comes from the idea that we become the average of the five people closest to us. So we set out to build things that help you gather the right people around you.

Our first product is minti.

minti is real friend social: an app that turns time spent with friends in real life into memories you keep. Flip your phone screen-down, and minti counts the time you’re together, then saves each hangout as a little keepsake. Less screen, more connection.
