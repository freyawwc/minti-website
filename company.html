About

We become the average of the five people closest to us.

Our story

Building around real relationships.

Circle Five Studios is a technology company building apps that bring people together. We focus on social products designed around real relationships, not follower counts.

Our name comes from a simple idea: we become the average of the five people closest to us. The people you spend real time with shape who you are — so the tools you use to stay close to them actually matter.

Most social apps optimize for reach. We optimize for the opposite: fewer people, more presence. The right five, not the loudest thousand.

What we believe

A few things we hold to.

People, not metrics

We don't count followers or likes. We care about the hours you actually spend with the people you love.

Presence over reach

The best moments happen when the phone is down. We'd rather help you look up than keep you scrolling.

Small and warm

We're a tiny team who believe the right people make you happier. We build things we'd want our own friends to use.
