Circle Five Studios

Circle Five Studios is a startup company building social products around real relationships. We believe the people closest to you shape who you become, and we’re building things that help you spend time with them, keep them close, and remember the moments you share. We’re currently building minti, social, but for real life.

We believe technology should add to life, not replace it. Following someone is easy. Liking something is easy. Actually spending time together? Slightly harder. We’re building our company around the idea that relationships matter more than reach, presence matters more than performance, and the best memories often look like ordinary Tuesdays while they’re happening. We’re small by design, and we build things we’d want our friends to use.

We’re angel-backed by a group of serial entrepreneurs who have built Guitar Hero, Twitch, and Gamers.com, along with our lovely friends and families. We’re based in San Francisco and building for the people in front of you — the ones beside you, the ones you miss, and the ones worth remembering.

Freya Wu, Co-Founder & CEO
