why minti

be there now. remember it later.

Your phone captures everything — photos, messages, stories, streaks.

But it can't capture what it felt like to be together.

minti helps you make space for that.

Start a session with your friends, flip your phones face down, and be there.

Talk, laugh, eat, study, wander — whatever you came to do.

The timer tracks the time you chose to spend together. No likes, followers, or doomscrolling.

Just your people. Just being there.

the memory

make the moment count.

When your session ends, take a photo on your phone.

Everyone captures the moment from their own perspective, and all the photos come together in one shared session.

Together, they become a candy — a small memory from your time together.

Everyone gets the candy on their own phone and can drop it into a personal jar.

Sort your jars by people, places, eras, inside jokes — whatever feels right.

Because it's not just about what the moment looked like.

It's about what it felt like to be there.

how it works

01 · flip

Start a session with your friends and flip your phones face down.

The timer tracks the time you're actually together.

02 · capture

When the session ends, take a photo.

Everyone's photos come together in the same shared session.

03 · keep

Your session becomes a candy.

Drop it into any personal jar you choose — by person, place, era, inside joke, or whatever feels right.

04 · shake

Shake your jar to reveal a memory.

Poke your friends when you want to remind them of the good times you shared together.
