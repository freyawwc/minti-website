why minti

be there now. remember it later.

we take photos. send messages. post stories. keep streaks. our phones remember everything — except what it actually felt like to be together.

minti is a tiny ritual for changing that.

the problem

we have the photos. where's the story?

your camera roll remembers what it looked like. messages remember what you said.

but they don't really remember the five-hour hangout that wasn't supposed to happen. or the road trip where nobody knew where they were going. or the random tuesday that somehow became a core memory.

minti keeps those moments.

the swap

trade the scroll for the moment.

we're not anti-phone. we're not asking you to become that person who leaves their phone at home. just give real life a little more room.

start a session. invite your friends. flip your phones face down. the timer counts the time you actually spent together.

no likes. no followers. no doomscrolling. just you guys.

the memory

then comes the good part.

when you're done, take a few photos together. your session becomes a candy — a tiny piece of that moment you get to keep.

come back tomorrow. draw one. over time, your candies become a record of the life you've shared.

oh. we've actually lived a lot together.

how it works

it's really simple.

01 · flip

put your phone face down.

invite your friends. start the session.

02 · be there

talk. laugh. eat. study. wander.

do whatever you were going to do anyway. just… be there.

03 · capture

before you leave, take a few photos together.

you'll probably want to remember this one.

04 · draw

tomorrow, you'll get a candy.

draw it. keep it. add it to your collection. do it again next time.

and eventually you'll realise these aren't candies. they're all the times you chose each other.
