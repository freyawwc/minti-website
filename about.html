why minti

be there now. remember it later.

we spend so much of our lives looking at screens. we take photos, send messages, post stories, keep streaks — and yet sometimes we look up and realise we can't say what we actually did together.

minti is a tiny ritual for changing that.

the problem

we have so many photos. but where's the story?

your camera roll remembers what something looked like. instagram remembers what you posted. messages remember what you said.

but none of them really remember what it felt like to be together.

the swap

trade the scroll for the moment.

minti doesn't ask you to delete your social media, or become one of those people who "doesn't use their phone."

start a session, put your phones face down, and the timer counts the time you actually spent together — not followers, not likes, not views.

the memory

because the best part is what happens after.

when you're done, take a few photos together. then your session becomes a candy — not another thing to scroll through.

a little piece of your friendship you get to keep.

how it works

it's really simple.

01 · flip

put your phone face down.

open a session. invite your friends. when everyone is ready, flip your phones face down. that's when the timer starts.

02 · be there

let the time count.

talk. laugh. eat. study. wander around. do whatever you were going to do anyway. just… be there.

03 · capture

keep a little piece of it.

before you leave, take some photos together. because you'll probably want to remember this one.

04 · draw

tomorrow, you'll get a candy.

draw it. your session becomes a little memory you can keep. do it again next time. and again. until one day you look at your collection and realise: these aren't candies. these are all the times we chose each other.

your friendship history

maybe this is what social should have been.

we have profiles for ourselves.

feeds for strangers.

followers for people we barely know.

but where is the place that remembers us?

the people you've known for years. the friend you met last summer. the person who was there during a really weird chapter of your life.

minti keeps your story together. every session adds another little piece. over time, you can look back and see:

oh. we've actually lived a lot together.

one day

one day, you'll wish you could go back.

not because you want to relive everything. just one ordinary night.

the dinner where everyone stayed too long.

the apartment where you used to hang out.

the summer when you saw each other every week.

you won't remember every conversation.

you'll remember that you were there.

minti is for those moments.
