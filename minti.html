The whole idea

Phones down. It's kind of the whole point.

You know the dinner where everyone's technically there but also… on their phone? Yeah. We built minti for that dinner. Flip your phone screen-down and minti starts counting your time together. That's it. The less you touch it, the better it works — possibly the only app that's rooting for you to ignore it.

Step 01 · Session

Gather your people.

One friend hosts, everyone else joins with a single code. Two of you or the whole table — scan a QR and you're in.

Host or join with one code.

Step 02 · Flip

Just be here now.

Screen goes down, the moment counts itself. The timer keeps going as long as your phone stays face-down — so be here now, minti's got the clock.

Less screen. More connection.

Every session

Every hangout, remembered.

Each session ends with a candy for your jar. Snap a photo and the minutes and the memories are saved together — a shared album that belongs to all of you.

The minutes and the photos, saved together.

Collection

Collect your time together.

Every session earns a candy for your Candy Book — dozens to discover, one hangout at a time. It's oddly satisfying to watch it fill up.

Turns out "we've spent 7 hours together" hits different when you can see it.

Your jars

A jar full of your favorite people.

Every candy goes in a jar. Sort them however you like — friends, BFFs, the work crew — then give it a shake and watch your collection grow over time.

Give it a shake.

Your circles

Find out who really shows up.

minti keeps track of every friendship, ranked by real time together. See who's been showing up most and how your closest circles are growing.

Followers count nothing. Friends count in hours.
