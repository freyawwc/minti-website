Why minti

Social, but for real life.

Most social products are built for the algorithm.

You collect followers, follow content, and keep scrolling. But followers aren't always friends, and likes don't show how much life you've actually shared with someone.

minti brings social back to real life.

It helps you spend meaningful time together, then keeps a record of the moments that made your friendship yours.

The problem

We follow people online. But where's the story of us?

Your friendships are made of real moments.

The first time you met. The late-night study session. The BBQ party. The random Tuesday hangout. The ups, the downs, and all the ordinary days in between.

But social media isn't built to remember that story. It's built to show you more content.

Your shared moments get scattered across camera rolls, chats, and forgotten posts — with no place to see the history of a friendship grow.

The swap

Trade the feed for time together.

minti gives your friendships a place in real life.

Start a session, invite your friends, and put your phones face down. The timer counts the meaningful time you spend together — not likes, views, or followers.

Every session becomes part of your shared history.

The memory

Your friendships, kept in candy.

Each hangout becomes a candy you shared.

Draw one every day and build a collection of the moments you've lived together. Open a friend's profile to see your story unfold — from your first session to the latest one.

The more time you share, the more of your friendship you keep.

And when you miss someone, poke them and revisit a memory you made together.

How it works

Three steps. One shared history.

01

Flip

Put your phone face down.

Open or host a session and invite your friends to join. When everyone is ready, flip your phones face down and start the timer.

02

Capture

Be there, together.

Let the timer count your meaningful time together. Talk, laugh, study, celebrate, or simply enjoy being in the same place. When you're done, take photos together to capture the moment.

03

Draw

Turn the moment into a candy.

Your session becomes a candy you shared with your friends. Come back each day to draw one, collect your memories, and see your friendship history grow over time.
